## **Launch your idea today with Bolt + Supabase**

**This is a hands-on workshop led by Ted Patterson & Emanuel Perez**
This is a hands-on, beginner-friendly workshop where you’ll take your ideas from concept to prototype — all in one evening. We’ll guide you step-by-step through researching your website or app idea, building your first working prototype using **Bolt** and **Supabase**, and deploying it live so you can start testing right away.

**What to Expect**

* A quick intro to **Vibecoding** — how AI can accelerate your creative process
* Guided walkthroughs led by **Ted Patterson** and **Emanuel Perez**
* Hands-on building with **Bolt** (for rapid UI) and **Supabase** (for database, auth, and APIs)
* Real-time help from mentors and community members
* End the night with your **own live prototype** ready to share and test

**FAQs**
**What is Vibecoding?**
It’s a new way to build software using natural language, AI, and no-code tools. You describe what you want — the tools help make it real. No traditional coding required.

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No experience needed! If you can describe what you want to build, you’re ready.

**What should I bring?**
Bring a laptop. We’ll provide Wi-Fi, outlets, and snacks.

**Is this for beginners?**
Yes! Whether it’s your first project or your tenth, you’ll walk away with something new — and running.

**Is this free?**
Yes, but space is limited. Be sure to RSVP early.

**Is parking available?**
Yes. Town Center has five garages with more than 4,380 spaces and 86 on-street spaces available for public use.

[Get more info →](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/city-property/parking/town-center-parking)
