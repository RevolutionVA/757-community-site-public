- **Group:** Hampton Roads Cyber Security for Control Systems
- **Link:** [Event Link](https://www.meetup.com/norfolk-cyber-security-for-control-systems/events/317312707/)

**Description:**
**[Cybersecurity in America’s Water Industry: Are We Asking the Right Questions?](https://events.zoom.us/ev/AkPBo5EIbae7MMI6sp_7_DbtWUCepxD72B4C_GnH5NJ9hEgzznbH~AncnAgItqTo8ABKl4FLamodjhI8T2q6Yo_9QFzRjv6CF-h0IcHqRO4ZgfIgV8JTzbIUx27JBkRaZe8OiIYZubuvI1w)**

Cybersecurity threats to America’s water and wastewater systems continue to evolve, exposing vulnerabilities in an industry that provides essential services but often operates with limited resources. While awareness of cyber risks is growing, are utilities focusing on the right priorities?

This presentation examines the current state of cybersecurity in the US water sector, drawing on recent incidents to highlight
the real-world operational consequences of cyberattacks. It challenges conventional approaches to cybersecurity, emphasizing the importance of understanding operational risks, implementing practical and proportionate security measures, and preparing for incidents before they occur. Drawing on ISA/IEC 62443 and industry best practices, the session explores the questions utilities should be asking to move beyond compliance and build genuine operational resilience.
